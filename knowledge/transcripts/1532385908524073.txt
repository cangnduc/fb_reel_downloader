# UX Tips for Multi-Step Forms
2026-05-26 · https://www.facebook.com/reel/1532385908524073

[00:00] Twelve fields, one wall. Four steps, one path.
[00:03] The brain reads three fields without effort. Twelve in a row, trigger scroll fatigue.
[00:08] Chunking lowers cognitive load before it kills it.
[00:11] Progress shows where you are. Linear bar, numbered dots, step labels. Pick one.
[00:17] Users need to feel the end is close.
[00:19] Group fields by context, not by count. Personal, shipping, payment, review.
[00:25] Each step earns its own screen. Random splits feel arbitrary and slow.
[00:30] Validate inside the step, not at the end.
[00:32] Wrong email on step one shouldn't surface on step four.
[00:36] Block next when invalid. Inline red beats final-screen rejection.
[00:40] Save state on every step change. Back button preserves data. Refresh too.
[00:45] Lose the form once, you lose the user forever.
[00:48] Follow for more UX systems. Save this for your next signup flow.
