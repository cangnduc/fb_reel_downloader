# 5 Rules for Better Tooltips
2026-04-27 · https://www.facebook.com/reel/1280965717578919

[00:00] Your tooltip is annoying.
[00:02] Rule 1: Wait 300 milliseconds before showing. Instant tooltips fire every time a cursor passes by. That is noise, not help. Intent, not accidents.
[00:10] Rule 2: Point with an arrow. The tail connects the tooltip to its trigger. Without it, users cannot tell what you are explaining. Arrows remove all doubt.
[00:17] Rule 3: Flip near viewport edges. A tooltip cut off by the screen is worse than no tooltip at all. Detect the edge, flip the position. Detect viewport bounds. Adjust anchor.
[00:25] Rule 4: Dismiss everywhere. Mouse leave, Escape key, Focus out, tap outside. A sticky tooltip traps the user. Every escape route matters.
[00:32] Rule 5: Keep it tight. Maximum 300 pixels wide. One sentence maximum. A tooltip is a hint, not documentation.
[00:38] Save this before your next tooltip. Follow for more micro UX rules.
