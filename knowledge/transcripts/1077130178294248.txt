# Typography as a System
2026-09-19 · https://www.facebook.com/reel/1077130178294248

[00:00] Typography is a system. Same font, seven decisions between product and form.
[00:05] Five sizes max. One ratio, 1.25, never hand-picked pixels. 11 sizes collapsed to five.
[00:13] Three text colors do more than 10 sizes. Primary, secondary, muted. Color ranks inside a line, size ranks between blocks.
[00:22] Two weights do all the work. 400 and 600. When everything is bold, nothing is. Regular for reading, semi-bold for scanning.
[00:30] Line height moves opposite to size. 1.1 on headings, 1.5 on body. The same value everywhere is the AI-made tell.
[00:39] Letter spacing tightens above 24 pixels. -2%. Small uppercase labels open up instead. +5%.
[00:48] Body text stays between 45 and 75 characters per line. A paragraph stretched across 1400 pixels is unreadable. Cap the measure, not the container.
[00:58] Numbers in tables, prices, and timers use tabular figures. One CSS property: tabular-nums. Otherwise, the column wobbles on every update.
