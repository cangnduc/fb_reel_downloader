# UI Numbers: 5 Tips for Better Data Display
2026-08-25 · https://www.facebook.com/reel/28747148424888259

[00:00] Pro numbers line up, amateur numbers dance.
[00:03] Default fonts give every digit its own width. A one is thin, a zero is wide. Columns wobble.
[00:10] Tabular figures lock every digit to the same width. Turn them on and the decimals snap into a straight column.
[00:17] Right-align numbers, left-align text. The eye compares magnitude by the last digit, not the first.
[00:24] A thousand becomes 1K, a million becomes 1M. Full precision waits on hover.
[00:29] 14:32 means nothing to a human. 2 hours ago means everything. Relative for recent, absolute for old.
[00:37] Keep the currency symbol out of the number column. Align the decimals, not the dollar signs.
