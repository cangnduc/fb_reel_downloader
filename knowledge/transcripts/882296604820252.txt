# 5 Design Decisions that make Linear feel 'Expensive'
2026-09-12 · https://www.facebook.com/reel/882296604820252

[00:00] Why does Linear feel expensive? Five decisions. No designer.
[00:04] Decision 1: Density. 13 pixel text. 32 pixel rows. Letter spacing pulled in 1%. Twice the issues on one screen, no scroll. Density reads as competence.
[00:16] Decision 2: Borders, not shadows. A 1 pixel border at 8% white. Depth comes from three background values, not blur. Hover changes the surface value, not the elevation. Flat surfaces look engineered. Shadows look decorated.
[00:32] Decision 3: One color. Indigo on the selected row and the primary button, nowhere else. Status is an icon, not a colored pill. Everything else sits in gray.
[00:44] Decision 4: Keyboard. Every action shows its shortcut. C creates, Command K searches. Hover feedback in 80 milliseconds, transitions under 150. No bounce, no overshoot. The UI answers before you finish the gesture.
[00:59] Decision 5: Alignment. A 4 pixel grid. 16 pixel icons centered on the text. Labels left, numbers right, nothing centered. Alignment is invisible when right, loud when wrong.
