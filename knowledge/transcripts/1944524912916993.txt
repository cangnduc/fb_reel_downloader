# Form UX Timing
2026-07-01 · https://www.facebook.com/reel/1944524912916993

[00:00] The error fires while you're still typing.
[00:01] The timing is the whole thing.
[00:02] Validate only on submit and they fill 10 fields blind.
[00:05] Hit the button and everything turns red at once.
[00:08] Now they're scrolling up, hunting for what went wrong.
[00:11] Validate on every keystroke and you punish them mid-word.
[00:14] They type the first letter, you scream "invalid email".
[00:18] Technically correct, completely cruel.
[00:21] Validate on blur. Wait until they leave the field, then check it.
[00:24] They've finished the thought, so the feedback lands as helpful, not nagging.
[00:29] Once a field is wrong, the rules change.
[00:31] Switch that one to live and clear the error the instant they fix it.
[00:35] Don't make them submit again to find out they're forgiven.
[00:38] A green check is feedback too.
[00:40] Confirm the field is right, don't only flag wrong.
[00:43] Silence after effort feels like the form is judging them.
