# The Serial Position Effect in UI Design
2026-05-09 · https://www.facebook.com/reel/1159162060607416

[00:00] Your memory is U-shaped.
[00:01] The first item benefits from primacy, the last from recency.
[00:04] Items 2 through 8, they vanish.
[00:07] This is the serial position effect.
[00:09] Apply to your navbar: Logo first, conversion CTA last.
[00:13] The five items between them, most users won't recall them.
[00:17] So put your weakest links in the middle, your strongest at the edges.
[00:20] Apply it to landing pages: Lead with your strongest unique value prop.
[00:23] End with your strongest proof: testimonial, logo wall, case study.
[00:28] The middle is the skipping zone. Don't bury the gold there.
[00:31] Apply it to onboarding flows: Slide 1 hooks, Slide N pays off.
[00:36] The slides between get scrolled past.
[00:38] Move your filler to the middle, your magic to the bookends.
