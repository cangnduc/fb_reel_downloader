# Date Picker Design Best Practices
2026-05-18 · https://www.facebook.com/reel/1059078393502662

[00:00] Same date, 6 clicks or 1.
[00:02] Presets cover 90% of cases. Today, yesterday, last 7 days, last 30 days, last quarter. Custom range for the rest.
[00:12] Highlight the range. Hover paints a preview. Click once for start, click again for end. Drag the edges to refine.
[00:20] Show two months side-by-side. Ranges cross months. Don't make users click next four times. Three months on wide screens.
[00:27] Arrows move the cursor. Type the date directly. Enter validates, escape kills. Page up jumps a month, shift page up jumps a year.
[00:36] Mobile is not a popover. Full-screen sheet. Calendar scrolls vertically. Today stays at the top. Big confirm button at the bottom.
[00:44] Follow for more UI systems. Save this for your next form.
